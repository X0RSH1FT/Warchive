**Researched:** 2026-08-22  
**ZIP:** 30101. I used a conservative 7% tax estimate, making the pre-tax ceiling about **$4,672.90**.  
**Important result:** I found **no verified system** that includes white-glove setup, true mechanical lumbar adjustment, two mattresses, two bases, and tax for $5,000 or less.

## Recommendation

**Best Overall Within Budget: Sealy Cocoon Chill Memory Foam (2 Twin XL) + Sealy Ease Power Base (2 Twin XL)**

This is the closest verified fit under budget because Sealy provides free in-home delivery/setup, the Ease offers independent head/foot movement and Zero Gravity, and the Cocoon mattress is adjustable-base compatible. It does **not** have dedicated lumbar adjustment or a one-touch anti-snore preset; each side can instead be raised independently to an anti-snore head position.

Estimated package: roughly **$3,400 pre-tax / $3,640 with 7% tax**, assuming two $999 bases and two sale-priced Twin XL Cocoon mattresses. Confirm the Twin XL base/cart price before buying.

## Evidence And Risks

Purple is the only researched finalist with a verified, dedicated adjustable lumbar mechanism and all requested position presets. Its mattress is medium-firm, adjustable-base compatible, CertiPUR-US foam, Clean-Air Gold certified, and explicitly marketed for stomach/back/side sleepers. Purple’s base has a 4.5/5 average from 146 reviews, but it is non-returnable, so test its lumbar behavior in a store first.

Sealy’s Ease has 504 owner reviews averaging 4.7/5 and includes free in-home delivery, but reviews are hosted by Sealy and therefore are weaker evidence than independent testing. Sleep Foundation’s testing specifically found the Zero Gravity position helpful for lower-back discomfort, while noting its broad adjustable-bed guidance is not medical advice.

For a lumbar herniation, a clinician or physical therapist should guide sleeping posture. An adjustable base may improve comfort, but it is not treatment. Stomach sleeping often benefits from avoiding excessive softness and pelvic sink; test each mattress in your actual preferred position.
