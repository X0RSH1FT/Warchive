## Acquisition leads

- Starting weapon: sword and shield (Kliff) ([PS-BLOG]).
- Other weapons can be found while exploring (preview; no locations) ([PS-BLOG]).
- Vendors: Harpoon Gun at fishing docks after first acquisition; Eastern Witch's Fan at Witch's Workshop-Lyselia ([PATCH-2.05.00], [GT-WINGFAN]).
- Quest and activity rewards: Lumen Longsword (Garden of Trials), Harpoon Gun ("Where the Wind Blows"), Enhanced Fatum Blaster ("The Scattered Pieces"); see [quests.md](quests.md).
- Preorder and Deluxe Edition bonus items exist; named preorder gear is on [armor.md](armor.md) and in [items-materials.md](items-materials.md). Entitlement rules are in [overview.md](overview.md#editions-preorders-and-dlc-availability).

## Excluded prerelease claims

The Fandom Weapons article lists sword and shield, spears, greatswords, axes, bows, muskets and hand cannons, dual blades and daggers, and unarmed techniques. It says it relies on prerelease material. Its muskets, hand cannons, and dual blades entries are unverified and not recorded here as facts ([FANDOM-WEAPONS]). Its category page lists little and is not a completeness signal ([FANDOM-ITEMS]).

## Coverage limitations

- No base-game weapon was itemized; the 707 (Gaming.tools) and 557 (CrimsonDesert.app) totals conflict, and only 240 of the latter were exposed.
- The DLC index shows stats for 13 of 14 rows, and only at refinement +10; +0 values, requirements, and effects are mostly absent.
- Acquisition is stated for 3 of the 14 DLC rows (Lumen Longsword, Harpoon Gun, Enhanced Fatum Blaster) and for Eastern Witch's Fan.
- Base-game weapons could not be separated from free post-launch additions.
- The DLC index was readable once and redirected to an ad-sync endpoint on a later fetch; treat it as a community source.
- The official family list is broad and may omit types.
- Weapon upgrade rules: see [equipment-enhancements.md](equipment-enhancements.md).

## Patch-sensitive caveats

- Weapon availability and vendor behavior can change by patch; the Harpoon Gun vendor note is from 2.05.00.
- DLC index values were current at 2026-11-10, before 2.05.00 (2026-11-13).
- Early-game preview content (2026-03-04) may not match the current game.
