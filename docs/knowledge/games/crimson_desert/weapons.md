# Crimson Desert Weapons

Last checked: 2026-11-13 | Base game patch: 2.05.00 | Evidence: weapon families from official and platform-owner sources; named weapons very limited | Return to the [index](README.md)

Official sources give patch-level facts, not a weapon database. This list is not exhaustive, and no individual melee weapon name could be verified.

## Acquisition leads

- Starting weapon: sword and shield (Kliff) ([PS-BLOG]).
- Other weapons can be found while exploring (preview; no locations) ([PS-BLOG]).
- Vendors: Harpoon Gun at fishing docks after first acquisition ([PATCH-2.05.00]).
- Preorder and Deluxe Edition bonus items exist, but their names and gear types are not given; see [items-materials.md](items-materials.md).

## Excluded prerelease claims

The Fandom Weapons article lists sword and shield, spears, greatswords, axes, bows, muskets and hand cannons, dual blades and daggers, and unarmed techniques. It says it relies on prerelease material. Its muskets, hand cannons, and dual blades entries are unverified and not recorded here as facts ([FANDOM-WEAPONS]). Its category page lists little and is not a completeness signal ([FANDOM-ITEMS]).

## Coverage limitations

- No stats, requirements, or effects for any weapon.
- No complete taxonomy; the official family list above is broad and may omit types.
- Weapon upgrade rules: see [equipment-enhancements.md](equipment-enhancements.md).

## Patch-sensitive caveats

- Weapon availability and vendor behavior can change by patch; the Harpoon Gun note is from 2.05.00.
- Early-game preview content (2026-03-04) may not match the current game.
