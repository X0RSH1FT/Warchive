# Crimson Desert Bosses

Last checked: 2026-11-13 | Base game patch: 2.05.00 | Evidence: official for system-level claims; guide-reported partial roster | Return to the [index](README.md)

No publisher-maintained boss database and no authoritative total boss count were found. Every roster below is partial.

## Excluded or unverified claims

- A fan companion page claims 104 total bosses and gives per-boss difficulty, healing, drop, and respawn data. Its extracted table had inconsistent names and regions and contradictory counts. These claims are not recorded as facts ([FAN-BOSS]).
- IGN's side-boss list says it contains what has been "discovered so far" (updated 2026-06-30); it does not establish a total across story, field, DLC, or rematch bosses.

## Coverage limitations

- No complete roster, boss-to-location map, phase descriptions, or drop tables.
- Boss classification (story, side, field, DLC) is only available for the three guide entries, and only as "side".

## Patch-sensitive caveats

- Retry behavior changed in 2.05.00; older advice about restarting fights may be outdated.
- Rematches were extended after launch; the list of rematchable bosses is not given.
- Boss behavior may have been adjusted in patches not covered by the digest.
