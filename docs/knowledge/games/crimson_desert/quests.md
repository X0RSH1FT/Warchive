## Main story structure (guide-reported)

IGN's walkthrough (updated 2026-06-12) describes a Prologue, 12 main-story chapters, an Epilogue, and 39 "major quests", each grouping smaller steps, with individual walkthrough pages covering puzzles, combat, and rewards ([IGN-WALKTHROUGH]).

- Treat the 39 figure as the guide's structure, not as a total of all quests in the game.
- The guide predates patches through 2.05.00 and DLC; recheck before treating it as current.
- Chapter titles come from Game8 (see above); individual major-quest names from IGN were not captured.

## Related fixes in 2.05.00

Quest NPCs disappearing after opening Map "Details" during some quest progressions were fixed ([PATCH-2.05.00]). The same patch notes a Spire of the Sun puzzle fix; see [locations.md](locations.md).

## Coverage limitations

- About 6 of the tracker's 680 records are named individually; per-entry category, giver, prerequisite, reward, and missability are mostly missing.
- The tracker exposed only part of its underlying catalogue, and the other quest-index pages (Fextralife faction and side quest indexes, IGN, MapMaster, other interactive maps) redirected to ad or sync endpoints or could not be read.
- Fextralife's main-quest table (2026-05-15) did not establish full 12-chapter coverage; Game8's October index is newer but self-identifies as incomplete. Neither is a dated, reconciled base-plus-DLC catalogue.
- IGN's side-boss index says it lists bosses "discovered so far" (2026-06-30) and is not a quest index.
- Missables and unlock order are unverified.
- Version and platform scope is not consistently stated; sources are primarily for the Enhanced edition on PC and console.

## Patch-sensitive caveats

- The Greymane faction quest issue was live on 2026-11-13; platform scope was not specified.
- Quest-affecting bugs (Torstein, Pailune) were fixed in 2.05.00; earlier guides may describe workarounds that no longer apply.
- DLC quest unlocks follow 2.04.00 notes; the tracker's latest patch label (2.04.08) predates 2.05.00.
