## Abyss Gears versus Abyss Artifacts

These are different items and should not be treated as interchangeable.

- Abyss Gears: named gear/reward items (example: Parting Gift). Slots, stats, and what they apply to are not stated in official notes ([PATCH-2.05.00]). Community records add slots and effects for some entries (see the catalogue below).
- Abyss Artifacts: a progression currency for skills and stats ([IGN-SKILLS], [PS-BLOG]); a community record also says they can refine equipment ([GT-ARTIFACT]).

## Source difference: do Abyss Artifacts refine gear?

- IGN's guide says gear refinement also consumes Abyss Artifacts ([IGN-SKILLS]).
- A Gaming.tools item record describes the Abyss Artifact as usable to refine equipment, and the Eastern Witch's Fan record lists one Abyss Artifact in its +10 step ([GT-ARTIFACT], [GT-WINGFAN]).
- PlayStation's preview supports only character upgrades and new abilities ([PS-BLOG]).
- Status: the gear-refinement claim now has a guide and two community records behind it, but no official statement. Treat as Reported (medium); verify in game.

## Engraving or equivalent

No reviewed source confirms an engraving system or a clearly equivalent one. Enhancement levels and Abyss Gears do not establish that either is engraving. Status: unverified, not shown to be absent.

## Bonus item claim flow (Xbox)

The resolved Xbox notice says to update to 2.05.00, open Menu > Others > Options > Add-on, refresh, and claim rewards from the list. This concerns reward claiming on Xbox, not a general acquisition system ([XBOX-NOTICE]). Which items belong to which preorder or edition is covered in [overview.md](overview.md#editions-preorders-and-dlc-availability).

## Coverage limitations

- No official enhancement guide; the Abyss Artifact refinement claim comes from a guide and community records and conflicts in scope with the preview.
- The Abyss Gear list is partial: four DLC-index rows plus Parting Gift and Ancient Reckoning; the Gaming.tools Abyss category (499) was not itemized.
- Platform scope of the multi-account reset issue was not detailed.

## Patch-sensitive caveats

- Reset bugs were reported fixed in 2.05.00 (2.04.08 for the multi-account case); the exact behavior of earlier versions was not documented.
- Wagon crafting costs changed in 2.05.00; any older number is stale.
