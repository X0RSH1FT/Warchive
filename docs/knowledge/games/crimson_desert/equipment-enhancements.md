# Crimson Desert Equipment Enhancements and Crafting

Last checked: 2026-11-13 | Base game patch: 2.05.00 | Evidence: official notes confirm enhancement levels exist; no mechanics documented | Return to the [index](README.md)

Official sources here are bug-fix and change notes. They confirm that systems exist but not how to use them. No enhancement costs, limits, or recipes were found.

## Confirmed

| System | What is confirmed | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Enhancement levels | Equipment has enhancement levels. A 2.05.00 fix addressed a save/load bug that reset levels on certain items, including preorder-exclusive and Deluxe Edition bonus items, for accounts without access to them. | [PATCH-2.05.00] |
| Enhancement reset (multi-account) | 2.04.08 (2026-11-06) addressed an equipment-enhancement reset issue when one save file is used across multiple accounts | [PATCH-2.04.08] |
| Equipment strengthening as a feature | Named in official store text and presented in an official prerelease overview of combat and progression | [STEAM-BASE], [FEATURES-2] |
| Crafting Station | Canine Charter's Diving Helm and Diving Suit can be crafted there as of 2.05.00; ingredients not listed | [PATCH-2.05.00] |
| Wagon crafting | 2.05.00 adjusted material requirements for wagon equipment; no recipes | [PATCH-2.05.00] |
| Abyss Gears | 2.05.00 added Abyss Gears to rewards for raising Trust with witches and fixed a functional issue with the Abyss Gear named Parting Gift | [PATCH-2.05.00] |

## Abyss Gears versus Abyss Artifacts

These are different items and should not be treated as interchangeable.

- Abyss Gears: named gear/reward items (example: Parting Gift). Slots, stats, and what they apply to are not stated ([PATCH-2.05.00]).
- Abyss Artifacts: a progression currency for skills and stats ([IGN-SKILLS], [PS-BLOG]).

## Source difference: do Abyss Artifacts refine gear?

- IGN's guide says gear refinement also consumes Abyss Artifacts ([IGN-SKILLS]).
- PlayStation's preview supports only character upgrades and new abilities ([PS-BLOG]).
- Status: unresolved. Treat the gear-refinement claim as guide-reported (medium to low confidence) and verify in game.

## Engraving or equivalent

No reviewed source confirms an engraving system or a clearly equivalent one. Enhancement levels and Abyss Gears do not establish that either is engraving. Status: unverified, not shown to be absent.

## Open questions

- Whether weapon and armor upgrades share a system or have separate paths.
- Enhancement requirements, costs, limits, and failure or reset behavior beyond the cited bugs.
- Recipes and materials for diving gear, wagon gear, and other craftable equipment.
- Equipment-specific DLC or event reward names.

## Bonus item claim flow (Xbox)

The resolved Xbox notice says to update to 2.05.00, open Menu > Others > Options > Add-on, refresh, and claim rewards from the list. This concerns reward claiming on Xbox, not a general acquisition system ([XBOX-NOTICE]).

## Coverage limitations

- No official enhancement guide; the guide claim on Abyss Artifacts is the only mechanic-like statement and it conflicts in scope with the preview.
- Platform scope of the multi-account reset issue was not detailed.

## Patch-sensitive caveats

- Reset bugs were reported fixed in 2.05.00 (2.04.08 for the multi-account case); the exact behavior of earlier versions was not documented.
- Wagon crafting costs changed in 2.05.00; any older number is stale.
