## Coverage limitations

- 14 rows against VULKK's 86 pet entries (different measures; not a coverage ratio); only two pets are named from that index (both DLC-tagged). VULKK's DLC tag is a database marker, not an established paid-DLC entitlement; see [overview.md](overview.md#editions-preorders-and-dlc-availability).
- No full roster, taming methods, stats, or pet abilities; effects are recorded only for dogs with Sigil of Valor and birds with Hunter's Sigil.
- Pywel's pet history is fan-maintained and stops at 1.14.

## Patch-sensitive caveats

- The digest covers only the first 17 patches (through 1.16); later pet additions beyond the DLC are unknown.
- Pet systems were extended after launch (resting in 1.13), so launch-era guide content may be stale.
- Canine Charter's gear became craftable in 2.05.00.
