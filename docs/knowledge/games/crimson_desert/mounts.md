## Counts

The update digest says its first 17 patches added 120 new mounts and pets collectively. It is not 120 of each, and not a definitive total ([UPDATE-HIGHLIGHTS]).

## Taming and acquisition

IGN's beginner guide (2026-03-23) describes taming and registering wild horses. Exact steps and values may have changed with patches; none are recorded here ([IGN-TIPS]).
