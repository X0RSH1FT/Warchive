## Counts

The update digest says its first 17 patches added 120 new mounts and pets collectively. It is not 120 of each, and not a definitive total ([UPDATE-HIGHLIGHTS]). VULKK reports 63 mount and ship records ([VULKK-MOUNTS]); Gaming.tools reports 49 Mounts knowledge entries ([GT-KNOWLEDGE]). These are different taxonomies, not proof either count is wrong.

## Related transport (not mounts)

- Wagons: equipment for them is craftable, and 2.05.00 changed material requirements ([PATCH-2.05.00]).
- Ships: DLC feature with boarding, map display, repair, and cannon changes in 2.05.00 ([PATCH-2.05.00], [OFF-DLC]).

## Unverified lead

A Game8 "List of All Mounts" (search result dated 2026-11-02) redirected to an ad page and was not read; none of its entries are recorded ([GAME8-MOUNTS]).

## Coverage limitations

- Stats and mount equipment slots are not documented (see [armor.md](armor.md)); acquisition is stated for 3 of 8 individually named base mounts (locations only) and for 5 of 7 DLC underwater mounts (Pink Manta Ray is too vague and Sea Turtle is unconfirmed).
- The base list predates the DLC; VULKK's DLC ship tags do not demonstrate every post-release aquatic mount.
- Game8's mount list was unreadable; see below.

## Patch-sensitive caveats

- Summon rules changed in 2.05.00.
- The Garden of Trials and legendary-mount issues were live on 2026-11-13 and may be fixed.
- Ship prices and wagon-equipment materials changed in 2.05.00; DLC guide values from 2026-11-10 may be stale.
