# Crimson Desert Armor and Wearables

Last checked: 2026-11-13 | Base game patch: 2.05.00 | Evidence: official patch notes for four named pieces; no catalog | Return to the [index](README.md)

Patch notes confirm item names and specific changes but give no stats, slots, or full acquisition paths. This page is not a complete armor list.

## Named pieces confirmed in official notes

| Item | Category | What the source says | Version | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Canine Charter's Diving Helm | Headgear, diving gear | Can now be crafted at a Crafting Station; ingredients not listed | 2.05.00 | [PATCH-2.05.00] |
| Canine Charter's Diving Suit | Diving gear (slot not stated) | Same crafting change | 2.05.00 | [PATCH-2.05.00] |
| Constellation Helm | Headgear | Fixed an issue that prevented unequipping it while swimming | 2.05.00 | [PATCH-2.05.00] |
| Nibrak Cloth Armor | Cloth armor | Fixed an issue where it disappeared | 2.04.07 (hotfix, 2026-11-05) | [PATCH-2.04.07] |

## Categories

- Headgear: Constellation Helm and Diving Helm. A headgear-visibility option was added in 1.02 ([UPDATE-HIGHLIGHTS]).
- Cloth armor: Nibrak Cloth Armor is the only named example; no armor-material taxonomy was found.
- Diving gear: Diving Helm and Diving Suit.
- Outfits: the official digest says the first 17 patches added "70 new outfits, equipment, and items" combined. It is not an armor or outfit count, and items are not listed ([UPDATE-HIGHLIGHTS]).

## Unresolved

- Whether outfits are cosmetic or stat-bearing is not consistently distinguished in the sources. Check in-game descriptions.
- No accessory slots (rings, necklaces, belts, charms) are confirmed. The Axiom Bracelet appears only in prerelease wiki material and is excluded.
- Equipment slots, stats, and set bonuses: not found.

## Coverage limitations

- Four named pieces only; names from unverified wikis are not promoted.
- Fandom's Armor page returned no substantive catalog during research.

## Patch-sensitive caveats

- Item disappearance, unequip, and duplication bugs were fixed in the cited patches; behavior in earlier versions differed.
- Crafting availability for the diving gear began in 2.05.00 (earlier availability not stated).
