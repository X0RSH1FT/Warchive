## Abyss Gear wearables

Four DLC Abyss Gear items (gloves and footwear) and related entries are catalogued in [equipment-enhancements.md](equipment-enhancements.md#abyss-gear-catalogue-6-entries) to keep one owner for those facts.

## Categories

- Headgear: Constellation Helm and Diving Helm. A headgear-visibility option was added in 1.02 ([UPDATE-HIGHLIGHTS]).
- Cloth armor: Nibrak Cloth Armor is the only named example; no armor-material taxonomy was found.
- Diving gear: Diving Helm and Diving Suit.
- Outfits: the official digest says the first 17 patches added "70 new outfits, equipment, and items" combined. It is not an armor or outfit count, and items are not listed ([UPDATE-HIGHLIGHTS]).

## Unresolved

- Whether outfits are cosmetic or stat-bearing is not consistently distinguished in the sources. Check in-game descriptions.
- Accessory slots: the community DLC index lists cloaks, an earring, and a ring (see above). Earlier research found no confirmed accessory slots; treat the slot names as community-reported. The Axiom Bracelet appears only in prerelease wiki material and is excluded.
- Equipment slots, stats, and set bonuses for base-game gear: not found.

## Coverage limitations

- Official notes name four pieces; the 17 other entries are community-reported DLC names.
- Piece-level contents, slots, acquisition, and set bonuses are missing for all 12 sets.
- Names from unverified wikis are not promoted. Fandom's Armor page returned no substantive catalog during research.
- The Armor 2,257 and Accessories 84 counts in Gaming.tools were not itemized and overlap other categories.

## Patch-sensitive caveats

- Item disappearance, unequip, and duplication bugs were fixed in the cited patches; behavior in earlier versions differed.
- Crafting availability for the diving gear began in 2.05.00 (earlier availability not stated).
- DLC index values were current at 2026-11-10, before 2.05.00.
