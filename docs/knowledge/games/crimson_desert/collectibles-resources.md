## Fishing and gathering

- No complete list of fish, gatherables, or nodes was verified. A search lead claimed 56 aquatic-knowledge entries; it was not confirmed from a directly inspected fish index and is not recorded as a count.
- Gaming.tools reports 161 Gatherables records ([GT-KNOWLEDGE]).
- The features exist in the official store text; see [systems-progression.md](systems-progression.md).

## Coverage limitations

- Subtype and category rows are counts only. No collectible, fish, or gatherable is named individually except the entries above.
- The knowledge indexes do not separate base-game from DLC records.
- DLC marker counts predate 2.05.00 and are partial.

## Patch-sensitive caveats

- 2.05.00 changed ambergris placement and sea creature density ([locations.md](locations.md)); marker counts from 2026-11-10 may be stale.
- Pioneering missions on Phragma and Estra are construction prerequisites in 2.05.00.
