## Relationships and Trust (DLC-dependent)

- *Charting the Unknown* introduces deeper NPC bonds ([STEAM-DLC], [OFF-DLC]).
- IGN's Romance Guide (updated 2026-10-30, still being updated) describes flirting, marriage, and NPC Trust as distinct mechanics, and says romantic Trust can decrease with other playable characters after flirting ([IGN-ROMANCE]). Medium confidence; NPC eligibility is not complete.

## Patch-sensitive caveats

The Known Issues notice (updated 2026-11-13) says Greymane faction quests cannot progress while playing as Damiane or Oongka, and faction quest information may display incorrectly ([KNOWN-ISSUES]). Treat as a live bug, not permanent design. See [quests.md](quests.md).

## Coverage limitations

- No official roster of comrades, factions, leaders, or NPC biographies; 6 named NPC rows against 465 source-reported VULKK NPC records (different measures; not a coverage ratio).
- Character progression timing comes from a launch-era guide.
- DLC NPC and ocean faction coverage could not be reconciled against the 465 NPC entries.
