# Crimson Desert Recipes and Crafting

Last checked: 2026-11-13 | Base game patch: 2.05.00 | Evidence: community item records and a cooking guide; official notes for crafting-station facts | Return to the [index](README.md)

Researched catalogue, not an exhaustive one. Recipes were found only as fragments inside item records, so most rows give the output and the partial inputs the record showed. No recipe table was inspected end to end.

## Cooking recipes

Rock Paper Shotgun says there are nearly 100 cooking recipes and lists base recipes, ingredients, and some effects, but the individual recipes were not carried into the research reports. They are therefore not listed here. The guide excludes quality variants and leaves some ingredients and effects blank, and does not separate DLC recipes ([RPS-RECIPES]). Fish Fillet appears as a feeding item for DLC mounts ([APP-DLC-UNDERWATER]); its recipe was not captured.

## Coverage limitations

- 23 recipe rows against 700 Crafting Manuals records and nearly 100 cooking recipes: the catalogue is a small fraction of the reported totals.
- Ingredient quantities, station requirements, unlock conditions, and costs are missing for almost every row.
- Whether the Aeserion weapons and Eastern Witch's Fan are base-game or DLC is not established.
- The Gaming.tools records are community data, not official.

## Patch-sensitive caveats

- Wagon-equipment material amounts changed in 2.05.00 without published values.
- Canine Charter's gear became craftable in 2.05.00; earlier availability is not stated.
