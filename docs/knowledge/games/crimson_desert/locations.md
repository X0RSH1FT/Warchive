## Quest-linked places (guide)

Hernand, Pailune, Demeniss, Delesyia, and Scholastone appear as settings in IGN walkthrough quests ([IGN-WALKTHROUGH]). Pailune also appears in an official patch note ([PATCH-2.05.00]; see [quests.md](quests.md)). Use the guide for quest context only, not as the authority on regions.

## Navigation aids (low to medium confidence)

The fan-made Crimson Desert Hub map advertises filters for quests, towns, bosses, resources, facilities, caves, and other markers, and claims over 2,000 marked locations ([FAN-MAP]). These are the site's own claims; marker accuracy and completeness were not verified. Use it to find candidates only.

## Coverage limitations

- No complete region list, atlas, or coordinates; the 14,300+ and 2,000+ marker claims are the sites' own and cover resources and points of interest as well as places.
- Place types above are inferred only from how the sources mention them; verify in game.
- Most island rows are names only; no settlement, interior, or dungeon catalogue exists in the research, and the housing guide's residence list is not reproduced here (see [systems-progression.md](systems-progression.md)).
- Whether Cloven Hoof Isle is an island (17 versus 18 count) is unresolved.
- The Greymane camp is a game feature ([systems-progression.md](systems-progression.md)); its location is not described in the research.

## Patch-sensitive caveats

- Arimuzar Village was relocated in 2.05.00; older guides may show the previous position.
- Hostile density, item placement, and construction prerequisites were changed in 2.05.00.
- The island guide and Great Ocean map were updated 2026-11-10, before 2.05.00.
